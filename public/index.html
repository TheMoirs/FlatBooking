The Flat

Our holiday home is in Calahonda, Costa del Sol, on the south coast of Spain — about halfway between Malaga and Marbella. You'd fly into Malaga; it's about a 30-minute drive from the airport, and while we'd suggest hiring a car, it's not essential.

It's a 3-bed apartment, with 3 double beds that split into 6 singles, plus a double sofa bed in the lounge — so we can sleep 6 comfortably, or 8 at a push. Air conditioning is fitted in all bedrooms and the lounge-diner, though it's really only needed in July and August.

Our terrace overlooks the shared pool with lovely views over Calahonda to the sea — on a clear day you can see Gibraltar and North Africa.
